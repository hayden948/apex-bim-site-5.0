FAQ

Pricing FAQ

What counts as a generation?
A generation is a single AI-authored Revit family produced from one source input, such as a submittal PDF or shop drawing. Re-running validation, exporting survey points, or syncing an already-generated family does not consume an additional generation. Minor parameter edits and re-saves are always free.
Can I switch plans?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings, and changes take effect immediately. Upgrades are prorated for the remainder of your billing period, and any unused credit is applied to your next invoice automatically.
Is there a free trial?
Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You get the full Starter feature set plus a generous batch of generations so you can validate the workflow on a real project. If it is not a fit, simply let the trial lapse and you will not be charged.
How does pricing work?
We offer both monthly and annual plans, with a discount for annual commitments. We're finalizing public pricing right now — book a demo and we'll put together a plan and quote tailored to your team's size, disciplines, and volume.
What about enterprise security & SSO?
Enterprise and Custom plans include SSO / SAML, role-based access controls, and audit logging. For regulated programs we offer on-prem or VPC deployment, a full security review, and a signed SLA. Reach out and our team will walk your security stakeholders through our controls and documentation.
